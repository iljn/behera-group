Our work connects materials synthesis, structure, and function across three closely related directions.

### Atomically precise synthesis

We use atomic layer deposition to explore dielectrics and ferroelectrics, semiconductors, metal interconnects, superconductors, and their heterostructures.

### Ultrathin and ultrasmall limits

We investigate how structure and properties change as film thicknesses and grain dimensions approach the scale of a unit cell.

### Functional materials for future chips

We explore electric-field control of charge, spin, and crystal structure to develop new capabilities in logic, memory, thermal management, sensing, and quantum technologies.
